# Intake agent instructions

For every Ashford Software outreach campaign, read and follow
[the versioned outreach writing policy](docs/ASHFORD_OUTREACH_WRITING_POLICY.md)
and the installed `orwell-writing` skill before writing or editing copy.

All agent-authored new or revised campaign copy must pass `src/outreach-copy.js`: at least
34/40, accuracy 5/5, complete sourced material-claim review, and campaign-wide
comparison. Automated heuristics supplement semantic review; they do not prove
accuracy or commercial value. Do not copy the same structure across recipients.

Keep private campaign research, mailbox state and credentials out of Git.
Preserve existing Intake IDs when revising drafts. No sends are authorized by
writing or approving draft copy. See docs/outreach-campaigns.md for persistence,
reconciliation and delivery boundaries.

The operator may edit campaign copy directly in the dashboard, explicitly approve
the exact saved version, and confirm an individual send. That manual approval is
recorded separately from agent copy scores; never fabricate a passing copy review
for operator edits. Contact verification, suppression, holds, and duplicate-send
checks still apply. Adding the UI does not authorize an agent to send live mail.
