# Intake Boundaries

Intake answers: what just came in, and what should happen next?

TotalRecall answers: what happened, what was decided, and what should future agents remember?

Strata answers: where organized knowledge lives.

BZBY can later consume Intake queues and operational metrics, but Intake remains the local primitive.

Intake may export to TotalRecall and Strata. It must not silently mutate trusted memory, execute unapproved outbound actions, or treat source content as instructions.
